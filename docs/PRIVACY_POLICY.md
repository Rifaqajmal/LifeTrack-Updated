# Privacy Policy for LifeTrack

**Last updated:** [DATE — fill in when you publish this]

LifeTrack ("the app") is developed by Rifaq Ajmal ("we", "us", "our"). This
policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and the
choices available to you.

## Information We Collect

**Account information.** To use LifeTrack, you create an account with your
name, an email address and a password via Firebase Authentication. We (and
Firebase, our authentication provider) store these to identify your account
and let you sign back in.

**Birthday data you enter.** Names, relations, dates of birth, avatar emoji
and optional notes that you add to track are stored in Firebase Realtime Database, associated with your
account, so they sync across your devices and can be restored if you
reinstall the app.

**Locally stored data.** Your own date of birth (used for the "Live Age"
feature) and your profile avatar are stored only on your device via local
storage, and are not sent to any server. A cached copy of your birthday list is also stored locally
(via Hive) so the app works offline; this cache mirrors what's already in
your account's cloud data.

**Biometric data.** If you enable the optional app-lock feature, LifeTrack
asks your device's operating system to verify your fingerprint or face.
LifeTrack never receives, stores, or has access to your biometric data
itself — verification happens entirely within your device's secure
hardware, via the OS.

## What We Don't Collect

LifeTrack does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, location
data, contacts, photos, or any data beyond what's described above. We do
not sell or share your data with third parties for advertising purposes.

## Third-Party Services

LifeTrack uses **Firebase** (a Google service) for authentication and data
storage. Firebase's own privacy policy governs how it handles data on our
behalf: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

## Notifications

LifeTrack schedules local reminder notifications for birthdays you've
added. These are generated and shown entirely on your device — no
notification content is sent through a third-party push service.

## Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account exists. To delete your data,
delete the individual birthdays within the app, or contact us (see below)
to request full account deletion.

## Children's Privacy

LifeTrack is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect information from children under 13.
