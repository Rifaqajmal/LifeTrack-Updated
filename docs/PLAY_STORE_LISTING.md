## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Never forget a birthday again. LifeTrack keeps every birthday you care
about in one place, with reminders, fun stats, and a beautiful, simple
design.

**Track every birthday in one place**
Add birthdays for friends, family, and anyone else you want to remember —
with their name, relation, and date of birth. LifeTrack syncs your list
across devices and even works offline.

**Never miss a celebration**
Get a reminder the day before and on the actual birthday, so you always
have time to reach out.

**See your own live age, down to the minute**
LifeTrack's live age calculator shows your exact age in years, months,
days, and hours — updating in real time.

**Fun life stats**
For every birthday, see playful stats like estimated heartbeats, breaths
taken, and hours slept since they were born.

**Share the moment**
Generate a birthday wish message or a shareable birthday card image, and
send it directly from the app.

**Privacy-friendly**
LifeTrack only stores what it needs to work — your account email and the
birthdays you add. No ads, no trackers, no analytics.

**Secure and personal**
Lock the app with your fingerprint or face for extra privacy on shared
devices.

**Works your way**
Choose light or dark mode, or let LifeTrack follow your system setting.
